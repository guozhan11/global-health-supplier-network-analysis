How to use these results

Confirm the need first

WHO's invitations show which medicines are priorities for prequalification. They do not show a shortage in any country, and one or two suppliers may already meet demand.

A list to investigate

One buyer's view

PQR only shows Global Fund-financed purchases. A firm may already sell a medicine to other buyers, and 2025-2026 data are incomplete because of reporting lags.

Check WHO listings by hand

The WHO match compares molecule combinations. It does not check strength or dosage form, which matters most for child formulations.

Capability is one input

Sharing suppliers suggests related production capability. Price, demand and a firm's own plans also decide whether it enters.
